01 / FEATURED PROJECT

Blizzard.

One menu for your Wii payloads. Keep your DOL and ELF files on an SD card, list them in payloads.xml, and choose what to launch.

Use the Homebrew Channel app for everyday sessions, or the standalone payload with an existing compatible launch method.

Why this exists

When projects get abandoned, a scene can start to feel like its heartbeat is slowing down. Blizzard comes from wanting to do something about that, even on a small scale.

This site brings the project, its source, and its setup instructions into one place. More useful than a maintenance screen. More welcoming than a 404.
